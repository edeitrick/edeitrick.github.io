Evolve Coach

Evolve Coach gave engineers feedback on their own real work. I defined AI judgment as 28 observable behaviors, and we launched a coach inside Claude Code and GitHub Copilot in three months.

We validated it for five weeks with 19 engineers across 3,112 real-work opportunities, and they followed 54% of its feedback. It ran with zero data retention, a secrets scrubber, and evaluation harnesses on the coach itself.

How I build with AI

Claude Code is my default way to build. I am not a software engineer: I decide what a tool should do, write the requirements, and feed back what breaks. Claude writes the code.

Price Alert Emailer

A Chrome extension and web app that emails you when a saved product reaches your target price at any store you shop. It refuses to match look-alike products, and it asks before trusting a match it is unsure of.

Life dashboard

A private dashboard for my week. Each morning an agent reads my email, calendar, and notes, drafts what it can, and checks each item against its source. Nothing is sent or scheduled until I tap Approve.
